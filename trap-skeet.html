WELCOME TO THE RANGE

General Information

Shooters wishing to shoot at WSC must complete the following. Non-members are welcome.

  1. Familiarize themselves with the WSC Range Safety Rules.
  2. Review and sign the WSC Range Waiver Form annually.
  3. Register and sign in at the range shack prior to shooting.
  4. New members must attend a WSC Range Orientation and Safety Course at the earliest opportunity. A nominal one-time fee applies.
  5. Beginner and novice shooters are welcome — instruction is available by appointment.
  6. Range reservations are available for private functions, stags, hunting clubs, meet and greets, and more.

SAFETY COMES FIRST

Range Rules

SAFETY AND COMMON SENSE ARE IMPORTANT When in doubt, ask questions and check it out.
  1. Safety glasses and hearing protection are required.
  2. All shooters must have a valid PAL or be supervised by a PAL holder.
  3. No drugs or alcohol in the vicinity of the ranges.
  4. Skeet Range
    • Maximum 12-gauge; minimum barrel length 26 inches.
    • Only 2¾-inch shells, #9 shot.
    • Two shells allowed on Stations 1–7; one on Station 8.
    • Actions open until in position.
    • Tactical shotguns are not permitted.
  5. Trap Range
    • Maximum 12-gauge; minimum barrel length 26 inches.
    • Only 2¾-inch shells, #7½ shot or smaller.
    • One shell loaded for singles.
    • Tactical shotguns are not permitted.
  6. Maintain safe muzzle direction at all times.
  7. For a misfire or hang-fire, keep the action closed for 60 seconds, then inspect the barrel.
  8. When transporting firearms: prove safe, action open, barrel up.
  9. Obey all RSO commands.
  10. Firearms must be unloaded with actions open when moving between stations.
  11. Pick up spent hulls only after the firearm is in the rack.
  12. Remove slings and accessories before attending firing lines.
