Be Part of Something Great

Windsor Sportsmen’s Club has been a gathering place for outdoor enthusiasts and families since 1945. Whether you’re here to shoot, compete, volunteer or just enjoy great company, there’s a place for you at the club.

Originally the Essex County Sportsmen (1928), it was renamed the Windsor Sportsmen's Club (WSC) in 1932. We are a non-profit organization incorporated under the laws of the province of Ontario to establish a group of Sportsmen interested in sponsoring and promoting laws governing the conservation of lands, forest, game and fish and the prevention of pollution of streams, rivers, lakes, and air to promote the propagation of game and fish. The WSC is affiliated with the Ontario Federation of Anglers and Hunters (OFAH) and Multi-Lakes Conservation Association (Michigan).
